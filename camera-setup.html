Face emergency (beta)

When someone new turns to look at your screen, blender-blender switches to 100% good student tab. It fires once per new person: if it was a false alarm, press ⌘X to come back and it won’t fire again for that person.

It needs your camera. Video is checked on this computer only and never saved or sent anywhere. The camera is only on while a blend is running with Face emergency switched on (your camera light will be on then).
